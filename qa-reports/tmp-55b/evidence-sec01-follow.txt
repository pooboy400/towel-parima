=== دنبال‌کردن زنجیرهٔ redirect (-L) تا نقطهٔ پایانی ===
--- http://localhost:3000/admin/staff/
final=200 url=http://localhost:3000/admin/no-access
--- http://localhost:3000//admin/staff
final=200 url=http://localhost:3000/admin/no-access
--- http://localhost:3000/admin//staff
final=200 url=http://localhost:3000/admin/no-access
--- http://localhost:3000/admin/staff/..
final=200 url=http://localhost:3000/admin
=== x-forwarded-host جعلی ===
307 → http://localhost:3000/admin/no-access
=== همان ترفندها روی /admin/audit و /admin/settings ===
/admin/audit → 307 → http://localhost:3000/admin/no-access
/admin/settings → 307 → http://localhost:3000/admin/no-access
/admin/audit/ → 308 → http://localhost:3000/admin/audit
/admin/settings/ → 308 → http://localhost:3000/admin/settings
/admin/./audit → 307 → http://localhost:3000/admin/no-access
/admin/settings/.. → 308 → http://localhost:3000/admin
